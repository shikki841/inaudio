# Community guidelines

Inaudio welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be patient with questions, give specific feedback, and discuss the code without attacking the person who wrote it.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and repeated disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, reviews, or other project spaces. Respect others' privacy and don't share their personal information without consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock discussions, or restrict participation. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so will not expose private information or put someone at risk. Repeated or severe violations may result in a permanent ban.

Use GitHub's **Report content** option to report abuse privately. For less sensitive concerns, contact a maintainer through their publicly listed contact channel; don't publish private details in an issue. Maintainers are held to these same standards.
