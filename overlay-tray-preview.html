Recording overlay & system tray

Sixteen frames for the two native surfaces owned by the main process. Colours, radii, spacing and type are read straight from src/renderer/styles/index.css; no gradients, no new palette. Frames 1–11 are the overlay window, 12–13 the tray menu, 14–16 the new Settings sections.

Design preview · example states. Tokens, radii and spacing come from the existing stylesheet; the overlay adds no colour, font or dependency that the app does not already ship.

  • Radix: Tooltip, VisuallyHidden, Separator, Progress, Toggle, ToggleGroup, DropdownMenu, ContextMenu, Popover, Portal
  • Animation: motion/react only inside the overlay renderer
  • Electron owns: create, position, show/hide, opacity, mouse pass-through, destroy
